## Deviations from the paper (this is NOT a faithful reproduction of the paper's experiments)
- FD/finite volumes on 2^d unknowns (AGENTS.md prescribes FD) instead of FEniCS FE; Dirichlet node removed,
  Neumann end via half cell; c_1 at cell midpoints.
- Euclidean (nodal) inner products instead of the FE L^2 mass matrix (library assumes identity mass).
- Prior Laplacian boundary conditions are NOT stated in the paper; homogeneous Neumann chosen.
  Nodal covariance = A_h^{-1}/h (Green's function values; pointwise variance 0.1386, mesh independent).
- Sample sizes are far smaller than the paper's (N <= 640 vs 20 000 reference / 16 000 training; one
  NN run per configuration vs 10). exp6 bases use 160 samples.
- The TT/DIS-TT experiments themselves (exp1-exp5) are the note's checklist, not paper experiments.

## Semilinear tests
`tests/test_semilinear.py` (6): Newton converges (rel. residual < 1e-11, also at 30x forcing); paper
coefficients; JVP vs central FD (rel 1e-7); adjointness <w, Jv> = <J^T w, v> (rtol 1e-12) and batching;
linear limit second-order convergence against y = (s - s^2/2)/c; prior factor, KL modes and mesh-independent variance.
One mistake in my own first version of the prior test (free-space variance 1/(2 sqrt 20) used as the
target; Neumann images raise it to 0.1386) was fixed in the new test, not the library.

### exp2 bound check (89 s; semilinear d=8, exact directions, 200 test samples, bootstrap 20 x group 256)
- Exact training identity train_error == sum(tails): max rel gap 4.4e-11 over all 12 cases.
- Prop. 1 sandwich lower <= train <= upper held in all cases.
- Held-out test_error <= training upper_unprojected in 10/12 cases. FAILS for N=10 at eps=0.03
  (test 1.936 > upper 1.070) and eps=0.01 (0.1836 > 0.1732): expected, the unprojected sum is a TRAINING bound.
- Prop. 2 plug-in sampling term sum_k sqrt(2 r_k M_k / N) is 1.6e4-2.7e4 (N=10), 7.4e3-1.2e4 (N=40),
  2.4e3-3.9e3 (N=160), versus total ||Z||^2 ~ 1.8e3 and errors <= 220. "test <= upper + sampling"
  therefore holds 12/12 but is VACUOUS. M_k plug-in ~ 4.5e6 (N=10), 3.2e6 (N=40), 1.4e6 (N=160), not converged.
- test_error <= train + 2 * sum(bootstrap std) fails in several cases (bootstrap spread is a diagnostic only).
Full table: notes/T9_results/exp2_bound_check.csv.
